How a borehole happens

The drilling process
01
Stage — scroll
01

Hydrogeological Survey

Specialists assess the groundwater in your area and mark the best drilling location on your land.

02

Borehole Drilling

The rig goes down through soil and rock until it reaches the aquifer — the underground water.

03

Borehole Casing

The hole is lined and secured so it stays open, clean and productive for years.

04

Pump Installation

Your pump — electric or solar — is installed and tested, and the water reaches your tanks.
