Good to know

Questions people ask us

How much will my move cost?

It depends on the distance, the size of your move, access at both ends (stairs or lift), and whether you want packing, cleaning or clothes arrangement. Send us those details and we will quote on your actual move — not a generic price.

Which areas do you cover?

We take enquiries from across Kenya, including long-distance moves between towns and counties. Tell us your route and we will confirm how we can help.

Can you pack for me?

Yes — full packing and unpacking is one of our services, and it is the best way to protect fragile items. You can also choose part-packing for just the delicate things.

Do you clean the old house too?

Yes. We offer move-out cleaning for the place you are leaving and move-in cleaning for the new one, plus clothes arrangement so your wardrobes are sorted when you arrive.
