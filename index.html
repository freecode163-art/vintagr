About us

One team for the whole move — not just the truck.

Vintage Movers & Cleaners is a Kenyan moving company covering homes and offices across the country. Most movers stop at transport. We don't: the same team packs your things properly, moves them, cleans both ends, and even arranges your clothes in the new wardrobe.

Questions

FAQs — 05
How much does a move cost? +

It depends on the distance, the size of the move, access at both ends (stairs or lift), and whether you'd like us to pack and clean. Send your details on the quote form and we'll price your exact move — no obligation.

How do I get a quote? +

Fill in the form below and it opens WhatsApp with your enquiry already written — just press send. Or call us directly on 0183 061 155.

Do you pack for me, or do I pack myself? +

Your choice. We offer full packing and unpacking as a service, or we can simply load and move boxes you've packed yourself.

Which areas do you cover? +

We move people across Kenya — within Nairobi and Kiambu, and long-distance to towns like Thika, Nakuru, Mombasa, Kisumu and Eldoret. If your town isn't listed, ask — chances are we can still get you there.

Can you clean and arrange the new place too? +

Yes. We clean the place you're leaving and the one you're moving into, and we can unpack and arrange your clothes in the wardrobe so the house feels like home on day one.
